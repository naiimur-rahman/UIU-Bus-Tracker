Web & Android Background GPS Tracking

1. How the App & Web Platform Works

For Students (Bus Kothay): Track active university buses in real time on the interactive map with road-accurate speed, distance, and live ETA calculation.

For Drivers / Captains (Start a Trip): Pick your route (e.g., Notun Bazar ➔ UIU) and tap Start Driving to broadcast your live GPS to everyone.

Help Thousands of Classmates! ❤️

Whenever you are riding a UIU bus, simply tap "Start a Trip". Sharing your location takes just 5 seconds and helps thousands of fellow students track their bus and reach class on time.

2. Android Background & Lock Screen Setup

Important

Modern Android aggressively puts apps to sleep when the screen locks. Follow these 3 quick steps after installing the APK to ensure non-stop background GPS tracking:

1 Allow Location: "Allow all the time"

Go to App Info ➔ Permissions ➔ Location and choose "Allow all the time". This allows GPS updates even when phone is locked.

2 Disable Battery Optimization

Go to App Info ➔ Battery and select "Unrestricted" or "Don't optimize" (For Xiaomi/MIUI/Vivo/Oppo: enable "Autostart" and set Battery Saver to "No restrictions").

3 Keep Foreground Service Active

Allow notifications so the background service stays active in your status bar. Location sharing will continue uninterrupted even when the screen is locked or while multitasking in other apps!
